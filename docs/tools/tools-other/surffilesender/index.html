    SURFfilesender¶

    Overview

    This is the SURFfilesender page, part of Other Tools in the A-LIFE RDM collection.

    Authors: Irene Martorelli, Brett Olivier — Last modification: 2026-06-29 — Version: 0.1.0

    What is SURFfilesender?¶

    SURFfilesender is a secure file transfer service provided by SURF that allows you to share large files easily and securely. The recipient does not need an account to download files — they simply receive a link and can access the data directly. This makes it ideal for sharing data with external collaborators, reviewers, or anyone outside your institution.

    Available to all VU affiliated staff and students

    Log in with your VU institution account via SURFconext:
    filesender.surf.nl

    How to use SURFfilesender¶

    For step-by-step instructions on how to transfer data with SURFfilesender, see the dedicated manual:

    Transfer data with SURFfilesender

    Key features¶

    • Share files up to 1 TB in size
    • Works via your web browser — no software or plugins needed
    • Recipient does not need an account
    • Two sharing options: by email or via an anonymous link
    • End-to-end encryption available for extra security
    • Links are valid for a defined period of time, with a maximum of 30 days
    • Files are stored in the Netherlands

    When to use SURFfilesender?¶

    SURFfilesender is a good option in situations such as:

    • Your data needs to be reviewed for a journal — the journal requests access to your dataset during peer review, but your data is not yet publicly available
    • Your DOI is not yet created or not yet resolving — your dataset may not yet be archived or published, or the DOI exists but is temporarily not resolving due to a technical issue; SURFfilesender provides a secure temporary alternative while waiting for the issue to be fixed
    • You need to share data anonymously — the recipient should not know who is sharing the data, for example during double-blind review
    • The recipient has no account on Research Drive or Yoda — and creating one is not practical for a one-off transfer
    • You need to transfer a large file quickly — without setting up a shared folder or storage space

    Data sensitivity¶

    SURFfilesender is primarily used for sharing sensitive data that cannot be shared via open platforms. However, always verify that sharing the data via this service complies with your data management plan and any applicable agreements.

    Check your data sharing conditions

    Before sharing sensitive or personal data, make sure you are authorised to share it and that the recipient is entitled to receive it. If in doubt, contact the A-LIFE Data Steward.
