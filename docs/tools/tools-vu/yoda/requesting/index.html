    Getting Started with Yoda¶

    Overview

    This is the Getting Started page, part of Yoda → Storage Tools in the A-LIFE RDM collection.

    This document covers:

    • Requesting storage space in Yoda
    • Metadata fields for the request form
    • Login to Yoda

    Supporting materials: GEMs, Resources, FAQs
    Authors: Irene Martorelli, Brett Olivier — Last modification: 2026-06-22 — Version: 0.5.0

    1. Requesting Storage Space in Yoda¶

    To request a Yoda storage space for your project, fill in and submit the form via the VU Service Portal:

    Request Yoda Space form

    Important

    You must be affiliated with the VU and logged in with your institutional credentials to access the form. If you are not affiliated with the VU and you are part of the project, you can still have access to the Yoda Research Space, but permissions to access the space is via invitation from the the Group data manager of the Yoda space (see Manual How to manage access to group members Yoda Research Space).

    Before filling in the form, make sure you have the following information ready:

    1. Project Manager — full name of the head of the project
    2. Delegated person managing the data (if applicable)
    3. Project Budget number
    4. Estimated volume of data to be uploaded to Yoda

    2. Metadata Fields for the Request Form¶

    The request form contains a number of metadata fields required to complete in order to get accepted and for the Yoda space to be created..

    2.1 Filling in the Metadata Form¶

    To help you fill in all the fields of the form in correctly, we provide a best practices guide covering each field:

    Best Practices — Requesting Yoda Space

    2.2 Submit your form¶

    Once you have completed all fields, submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email within a few working days once your project space has been created.

    For any questions about the form, contact the RDM Functional Application Manager:
    Diógenes Cruz de Arcelino — d.cruz.de.arcelino@vu.nl
    or use the RDM contact form to submit your questions.

    3. Login to Yoda¶

    Once you receive confirmation that your Yoda project space is ready, you can log in to the Yoda Portal and start using your Yoda Research Space.

    3.1 Go to the VU Yoda Portal¶

    https://portal.yoda.vu.nl

    3.2 Provide your VU email address¶

    Sign in with your VU email address.

    3.3 Login with your institutional credentials¶

    Use your VU.nl credentials or eduID (NL).

    3.4 Select Research¶

    Select Research to access your active project space in Yoda.

    In this space you will be able to view your project folder. The project folder maps to the name you gave when requesting the Yoda storage space.

    Active storage = Research Space in Yoda

    The Research Space is the active storage area in Yoda where you work with your data during the project. It is the space you have just requested access to.

    What next?

    You are now able to start using your Yoda Research Space — go to Step 2: Organise your Data to prepare your files before putting them in the Yoda Research Space.
