    What is Yoda?¶

    Yoda is a research data management platform recommended at the VU that supports the entire research cycle — from the safe storage and sharing of active research data, to archiving and publishing completed datasets.

    In practice, Yoda serves two key functions in the research data lifecycle:

    • Active storage — a secure cloud space for storing and sharing data during your project.
    • Archiving & publishing — finalised datasets are submitted to the Vault, a read-only long-term storage space, where they can optionally be published and assigned a persistent identifier (DOI).

    Yoda Workflow¶

    Follow these steps in order to get started with Yoda and take your data from the active storage to publication.

    New to Yoda?

    If you have never used Yoda before, you must start with Step 1 — Request Storage Space before you can do anything else.

    Step 1 — Getting Started¶

    Before using Yoda, you need to Request Yoda Space for your project from your institution. In this case VU. In order to request it you need to fill in and submit the Yoda Request Space form for getting access to the VU Yoda Portal.

    Our documentation provides best practices to help you fill in the form correctly and permissions to get started:

    Getting Started with Yoda

    Step 2 — Organise your Data for Yoda Space¶

    Before uploading data to Yoda, organise your files locally first. Yoda is not designed for reorganising data after uploading — moving and renaming files inside the platform is less intuitive than doing it on your own computer.

    Organise your Data

    Step 3 — Transfer Data in Yoda¶

    Once your data is organised, you can start moving it into your Yoda Research Space. There are different ways to do this depending on your preferences and workflow.

    Transfer Data in Yoda Research Space

    Step 4 — Manage Group Members¶

    Follow this documentation for providing access and managing the members of your Yoda Research Space.

    Manage Group Members

    Step 5 — Metadata and Documentation¶

    Before archiving, complete the Yoda Metadata Form (mandatory) and add a README file to your data package folder (highly recommended). Without a completed metadata form, you cannot submit your data to the Vault.

    Metadata and Documentation

    Step 6 — Archive your Data¶

    When your files are finalised, archive your data in the Yoda Vault — a read-only, long-term storage space. Your submission goes through a data manager review before being accepted into the Vault.

    Archiving in Yoda

    Step 7 — Publish your Data¶

    Once archived, you can choose to publish your dataset. Published datasets receive a DOI and are made available through DataCite. VU Yoda published datasets can be browsed via DataCite Commons.
