    A-LIFE RDM documentation¶

    Your one-stop place for research data management support at A-LIFE. Whether you are just getting started, looking for the right storage tool, or ready to publish your dataset — we have got you covered.

    What you will find here¶

    Tools¶

    Documentation for the research storage, publishing, and data transfer tools used at A-LIFE:

    VU Tools¶

    • Yoda — storage, archiving and publishing
    • Research Drive — active storage and sharing

    Other Tools¶

    • Zenodo — open publishing and archiving
    • Cyberduck — desktop client for transferring data to Yoda and Research Drive
    • SURFfilesender — browser-based service for securely sharing large files

    GEMs — Guides, Essentials and Manuals¶

    A collection of practical RDM support materials:

    • Guides — step-by-step guides for common RDM tasks, such as filling in metadata forms or requesting storage space
    • Essentials — short reference documents on key concepts like PIDs, licences, and README files
    • Manuals — hands-on instructions for specific tools and tasks, including transferring data with Cyberduck, creating an eduID, and using SURFfilesender
